# Retired Workspace Material

This directory contains historical code, superseded or invalid documentation,
generated test results, backups and temporary working files.

The contents are retained for audit and recovery only. They are not current
requirements, calculations, thresholds, conclusions or work instructions and
must be ignored by normal application reads unless the owner explicitly asks
to reactivate or audit a specific artifact.

Archive categories:

- `Documentation` — superseded, invalid and historical documents;
- `Test Results` — generated runs, smoke outputs and prior research results;
- `Working Files` — temporary or one-off research scripts;
- `Legacy Code` — retired standalone scanner versions;
- `Input Data` — inputs belonging only to retired studies;
- `Backups` — point-in-time local safety copies; and
- `Generated` — caches and other reproducible generated material.

The active documentation-lifecycle policy and every future archive move are
recorded in the active V17 `CHANGELOG.md`.

`.worktrees\v17\docs\MOMENTUM_ENGINE_MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md`. Nothing in this
archive may override it.
