## Scope

## Current User-Facing Contract

`Score` is now the final user-facing confidence score and must not contradict `Final_Decision`.

- `Score = 100` means `MOMENTUM_ACTIVE`.
- `MOMENTUM_PRESENT_WAIT_CONFIRMATION` is capped at `84`.
- `REJECT` is capped below the default score threshold.

## Live Price Handling

V7 is now phase-price aware. When Yahoo provides a valid live quote in `PRE`, `REGULAR`, `POST`, or `POSTPOST`, the latest scoring bar is adjusted before indicators and final score are computed.

Audit fields added:

- `Regular_Session_Close`
- `Score_Price_Source`
- `Score_Price_Change_Pct`

Existing quote fields remain:

- `Market_State`
- `Live_Price`
- `Regular_Market_Price`
- `PreMarket_Price`
- `PostMarket_Price`
- `Extended_Hours_Change_Pct`

## Execution Log Versus Final Summary

V7 maintains two separate outputs:

- `Processed_Data/V7_Momentum_Execution_Log.csv` is the append-only, continuously updated audit log. Every processed ticker is written immediately with its full row, regardless of the final summary threshold.
- `Processed_Data/V7_Momentum_Execution_Dump.csv` is the final top-X summary written after the scan completes.

`--score-y` filters only the final summary. It does not filter the execution log and does not define the REJECT score cap.

All published scores are constrained to `0..100`. The fixed REJECT maximum is `49`, including when `--score-y` is set to `0`.

## Signoff

V7 is signed off for the current Stock Momentum Detector workflow with:

- Final score aligned to final actionability.
- Pre-market, regular-session live, and post-market price incorporated into scoring.
- Live timing able to downgrade otherwise strong momentum when the latest intraday/post-market evidence weakens.
